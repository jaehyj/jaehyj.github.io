    Plain English

    Every number, as a sentence.

    Conviction needs numbers you understand. Stokisy leads with what each one means and keeps the jargon as a small label.

    P/E ratio · 30

    You pay $30 for every $1 of yearly profit.

    Like buying a coffee shop that earns $1 a year for $30. It takes 30 years of profit to pay you back.

    Profit margin · 22%

    It keeps $22 of every $100 it sells.

    The rest goes to costs such as staff, rent and supplies.

    Debt-to-equity · 0.4

    It owes 40 cents for every $1 its owners put in.

    Lower means the company leans less on borrowed money.
